# Interaction summary logs

Summaries of the AI prompts and interactions that produced this project, as
required by the assignment.

## How these were produced, and how far to trust them

The summaries were generated by AI and then checked by the team. They are not
verbatim transcripts. Each file states which of two kinds it is, because the
difference matters when reading them as evidence:

- **Observed** — written by an agent that took part in the session, from its
  own record of the work. Claims about what was decided and why are
  first-hand.
- **Reconstructed** — assembled afterwards from the commit history and the
  artefacts under `workflow/`. The sequence and the outputs are verifiable; the
  conversational detail is not, and is not invented. Where a reconstruction
  cannot establish something, it says so.

Nothing here records a test result that was not observed. Where a suite could
not run, the logs and `workflow/features/*/tasks.md` say "NOT RUN" with the
reason.

## Verification

Each summary was checked against the commit history (`git log`), the feature
artefacts under `workflow/`, and the test output recorded in `tasks.md` and
`test-report.md`. Commit subjects and dates quoted in these files match the
repository.

## Relationship to the workflow artefacts

These logs describe *how the work happened*. The durable decisions, approvals
and evidence live in `workflow/`, which is the authoritative record. Where the
two disagree, `workflow/` governs.
